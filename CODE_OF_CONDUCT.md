# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions. Assume good faith, explain disagreements with evidence, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, publishing private information, and sharing recordings without permission are not acceptable. Do not post API keys, personal transcripts, or private voice samples.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, or restrict participation to protect the community. Consider the context and impact of a problem before responding. No one is entitled to another person's time or immediate support.

For a conduct concern, use GitHub's **Report content** control on the relevant contribution. For a security vulnerability, use the repository's private security reporting channel instead. Avoid repeating private information in a public report.
